Shithead

Shed every card — hand, then face-up, then the three you've never seen.

Players

House Rules

The goal

Get rid of all your cards. You play your hand first, then your three face-up cards, then your three blind cards — one at a time, sight unseen. First one out wins; play carries on for the places behind.

Setup this app's house rule

Everyone gets 3 blind cards face-down, then 6 cards in hand. Before play you choose exactly 3 of those 6 to lay face-up on your blind cards — those are your endgame. The other 3 are your starting hand.

Classic Shithead instead deals the face-up row at random and never lets you choose. Choosing is strictly better and much more fun, so that's what this app does.

A turn

  • Play a card equal or higher than the top of the pile. You may play several cards of the same rank at once.
  • Ace is high. Suits never matter.
  • While the stock lasts, top your hand back up to 3 after every play.
  • No legal play? You take the whole pile into your hand, and your turn ends. There's no voluntary pick-up here — if you can play, you must.
  • An empty pile accepts anything.

Special cards

2
Wild, and a reset. Playable on anything. The next player may then play anything.
10
Burns the pile. The whole pile leaves the game for good, and you go again on a fresh pile. Playable on anything.
7
Under seven. The next play must be 7 or lower — except a 2 or a 10, which are always legal. Normal rules resume after it.
8
Skip. The next player loses their turn entirely. Two 8s skip two players. house addition
4×
Four of a kind burns. Four cards of the same rank on top of the pile — even if they arrived over several turns — burn it. Whoever laid the fourth goes again.

Blind cards

With hand and face-up gone, tap a blind card to turn it over. If it happens to be legal it's played. If not, you swallow the pile (with that card on top) and you're back to playing from your hand.

Controls

  • Tap a card to pick it up; tap more of the same rank to stack them.
  • Drag onto the pile to play, or hit the play button.
  • Cards you can't legally play are dimmed.

First turn goes to whoever holds the lowest card in hand after the swap (ties broken by suit: ♣ ♦ ♥ ♠).
